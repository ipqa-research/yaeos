Rachford-Rice equation

Description

Utilities to solve the Rachford-Rice equation for the phase fraction of a two-phase flash, and to keep the K-factors within a range where a valid phase fraction exists.

Examples

call solve_rr(z, K, beta, beta_min, beta_max)
