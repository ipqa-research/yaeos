Nonlinear equation solvers

Description

Newton-type solvers for systems of nonlinear equations, including the damped multivariate Newton method newton. newton is the damped Newton method; homotopy gradually deforms an identity problem into the target system.

Examples

call newton(residual_jacobian, x, tol, max_its, iterations)
