Second-order isothermal flash

Description

Two-phase flash at specified temperature and pressure by direct minimization of the reduced Gibbs energy, following the formulation of Michelsen (1982).

The minimization variables are the scaled heavy-phase mole numbers , so the light phase is and the admissible region is . The Gibbs energy is minimized with a Newton step based on a modified Cholesky decomposition (Murray), combined with a line search that first computes the largest step compatible with the bounds and then backtracks on the Gibbs energy.

For ArModel the heavy phase is always evaluated with the liquid root. The light phase uses the stable root by default, but it can be forced to the liquid root with light_root="liquid" (useful for liquid-liquid splits). For GeModel, both phases are described by activity coefficients and the initial split is obtained from stability analysis.

Examples

result = flash_second_order(model, z, T, P)

References

  1. M. L. Michelsen, “The isothermal flash problem. Part II. Phase-split calculation”, Fluid Phase Equilibria 9 (1982) 21-40.
  2. W. Murray (ed.), Numerical Methods for Unconstrained Optimization, Academic Press (1972).
