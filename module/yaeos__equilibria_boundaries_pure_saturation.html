Pure-component saturation lines

Description

Calculation and interpolation of the saturation line of a pure component. pure_saturation_line traces the line from the critical point down to a specified pressure or temperature, and PurePsat stores and interpolates the result.

Examples

line = pure_saturation_line(model, component=1, minP=0.1_pr, minT=100.0_pr)
Tsat = line%get_T(P=1.0_pr)
