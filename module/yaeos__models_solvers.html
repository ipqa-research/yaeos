solve_point volume_michelsen

Model-specific numerical solvers

Specialized numerical methods used by EOS implementations.

Description

The principal routine is volume_michelsen. It solves in reduced covolume , bounded between zero and one, rather than iterating directly over an unbounded volume. Models must implement get_v0 to provide the covolume or lower-volume estimate.

Examples

call volume_michelsen(model, n, P, T, V, root_type="stable")

References

Michelsen, M. L., and J. M. Mollerup, Thermodynamic Models: Fundamentals & Computational Aspects, 2nd ed., Tie-Line Publications, 2007.
