Subroutines
pressure_equality_V_beta_xy

Fixed-volume two-phase pressure equality

Solve phase pressure equality for a two-phase state at fixed temperature and total volume.

Description

The routine treats the heavy-phase volume as the Newton unknown and obtains the light-phase volume from the overall volume balance. It returns the average phase pressure once the two phase pressures agree.

Examples

call pressure_equality_V_beta_xy(model, T, V, beta, x, y, Vx, Vy, P)
