PC-SAFT equation of state

Implementation of the non-associating PC-SAFT residual Helmholtz model.

Description

The module defines the model type PCSAFT, its parameter constructor init_pcsaft, and the primal and Tapenade-generated directional and adjoint derivative routines. PC-SAFT includes hard-chain and dispersion contributions but not the association contribution.

Examples

model = init_pcsaft(m, sigma, epsilon_k, kij)
call model%pressure(n, V, T, P)

References

Gross, J., and G. Sadowski (2001). Perturbed-Chain SAFT: An Equation of State Based on a Perturbation Theory for Chain Molecules. Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research, 40, 1244-1260.
