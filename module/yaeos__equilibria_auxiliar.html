P_wilson k_wilson

Phase-equilibrium initialization utilities

Wilson correlations for initial K-factors and pressure estimates.

Description

k_wilson provides component volatility ratios for initializing flash calculations; p_wilson estimates the pressure from a composition and temperature. These are empirical initial estimates, not equilibrium solutions.

Examples

K0 = k_wilson(model, T, P)
P0 = p_wilson(model, z, T)
