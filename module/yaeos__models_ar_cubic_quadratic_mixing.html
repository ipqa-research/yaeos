Quadratic cubic-EOS mixing rules

Binary quadratic mixing rules for the attractive and repulsive parameters.

Description

Exports QMR with constant binary interactions and QMRTD with temperature-dependent attractive interactions. QMR uses the classic pair combining rules and can be configured with component-pair matrices.

Examples

rule = QMR(k=kij, l=lij)
call model%set_mixrule(rule)
