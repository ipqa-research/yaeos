Saturation points

Description

Bubble, dew, and liquid-liquid saturation points at specified temperature or pressure. The module first iterates a scalar Newton method on with successive-substitution updates of , and falls back to a full Newton solve in if that does not converge. A non-converged result is returned with kind = "failed".

Examples

sat = saturation_pressure(model, z, T, kind="bubble")
sat = saturation_temperature(model, z, P, kind="dew")
