Numerical continuation

Predictor-corrector continuation of nonlinear solution curves.

Description

The module defines callback interfaces and a continuation driver for tracing solutions of as a scalar specification changes. At each accepted point the routine solves for the local tangent , predicts the next state, and corrects it with a nonlinear solver. The caller may customize the specification update, post-processing, point solver, and stopping condition.

Examples

curve = continuation(fun, X0, ns0, S0, dS0, &
   max_points=100, solver_tol=1.0e-8_pr)
