Three-phase PT envelopes

Continue a three-phase equilibrium boundary at fixed global composition.

Description

The module solves the three-phase isofugacity and material-balance equations and advances along the boundary with numerical continuation. Results are collected in PTEnvel3.

Examples

envelope = pt_envelope_3ph(model, z, x0, y0, w0, beta0, P0, T0, ns0, dS0, points)
