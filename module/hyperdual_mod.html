Hyperdual arithmetic

Type and overloaded operations for exact first- and second-order directional derivatives.

Description

A hyperdual value stores a primal value and two independent tangent seeds: The nilpotent rules make the Taylor expansion exact through the mixed second derivative. Overloaded arithmetic and elementary functions propagate these coefficients, so derivatives are obtained without a finite-difference step-size error. For a scalar function the propagation rule is Seeding , gives in f1/f2 and in f12; seeding different variables in and gives mixed second derivatives.

Examples

type(hyperdual) :: x, y
x = hyperdual(f0=2.0_pr, f1=1.0_pr, f2=1.0_pr)
y = exp(x)
print *, y%f0, y%f1, y%f2, y%f12

Bauer (2021). Application of Generalized (Hyper-) Dual Numbers in Equation of State Modeling. Frontiers in Chemical Engineering. DOI: 10.3389/fceng.2021.758090.
