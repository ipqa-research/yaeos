PSRK

Description

Predictive Soave-Redlich-Kwong (PSRK).

SRK equation combined with the modified Huron-Vidal mixing rule at and a PSRK UNIFAC excess Gibbs model. The alpha function is Soave by default, or Mathias-Copeman when c1, c2, and c3 are provided.
