public function arfun(self, n, v, t) result(arval)

arfun

Evaluate the GERG2008 mixture residual Helmholtz energy.

Description

Forms mixture reducing functions, computes reduced density and temperature, then sums composition-weighted pure-fluid contributions and binary departure terms. Hyperdual arithmetic supplies derivatives.
