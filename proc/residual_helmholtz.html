public subroutine residual_helmholtz(self, n, v, t, Ar, ArV, ArT, ArTV, ArV2, ArT2, Arn, ArVn, ArTn, Arn2)

residual_helmholtz

Evaluate residual Helmholtz energy and requested derivatives by AD.

Description

Converts real inputs into hyperdual variables, seeds their derivative slots, evaluates the concrete model’s Ar function, and copies the requested first- and second-order derivatives to optional outputs.

Examples

Usually called through inherited methods such as pressure or lnphi_pt rather than invoked directly.
