public subroutine excess_gibbs(self, n, t, Ge, GeT, GeT2, Gen, GeTn, Gen2)

excess_gibbs

Evaluate excess Gibbs energy and requested derivatives by AD.

Description

Seeds hyperdual composition and temperature variables, evaluates the concrete model’s Ge function, and returns requested first and second derivatives through the standard GeModel interface.

Type Bound

GeModelAdiff
