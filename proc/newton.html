public subroutine newton(sub, x, settings, result)

newton

Find a root of a dense nonlinear system.

Description

Solve with safeguarded Newton and LM fallback. Newton steps use Armijo backtracking; ill-conditioned Jacobians trigger Levenberg-Marquardt regularization. Diagnostics are returned even when the iteration does not converge.

Examples

call newton(residual_and_jacobian, x, settings, result)
