public subroutine solve_point(model, z, ns, S, X, F, dF, its, maxits)

solve_point

Description

Correct one point on the three-phase envelope with Newton iteration. Returns the residual, Jacobian, and iteration count at the corrected state; reaching maxits signals that the continuation step failed.
