public subroutine ArVnder(nc, nder, ntemp, z, V, T, ar, arv, artv, arv2, Arn, ArVn, ArTn, Arn2)

ArVnder

Dispatch the configured legacy residual Helmholtz implementation.

Description

Selects the active residual Helmholtz callback through ar_fun and returns the requested residual energy derivatives. NDER and NTEMP control the derivative sets supported by the selected implementation.
