volume

Find a PC-SAFT volume root at specified pressure and temperature.

Description

Delegates to Michelsen’s volume algorithm specialized for PC-SAFT. root_type selects the liquid, vapor, or stable root.

Examples

call model%volume(n, P, T, V, root_type="liquid")

References

  • [1] “Thermodynamic Models: Fundamental and Computational Aspects”, Michael L. Michelsen, Jørgen M. Mollerup. Tie-Line Publications, Denmark (2004) doi
