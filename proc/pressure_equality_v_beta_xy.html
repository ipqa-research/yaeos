pressure_equality_V_beta_xy

Solve pressure equality between two phases at a given temperature, total volume, vapor molar fractions and compositions.

Description

Uses to express Vy as a function of Vx, then applies Newton iteration to . The liquid volume is constrained to remain above the model’s covolume. A failure to converge within the internal iteration cap returns P=-1 after 100 iterations and writes a warning to the error unit.

Examples

See the module-level call pattern; x and y must be normalized phase compositions and beta is the light-phase fraction.
