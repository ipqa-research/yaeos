DmixHVNRTL

Huron-Vidal attractive parameter with the NRTL-HV model.

Description

For pairs flagged in use_kij, the NRTL-HV parameters are replaced (, gjiT=0, and gji0 from and kij) before evaluating and the Huron-Vidal relation.
