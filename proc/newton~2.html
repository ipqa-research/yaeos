public subroutine newton(sub, x, tol, max_its, its)

newton

Multidimensional Newton method with simple step damping.

Description

Solves with Newton-Raphson. Each step is repeatedly halved until its largest component is at most 0.1; this limits step size but is not a residual-based line search. its is the loop count and does not distinguish all convergence/failure cases.
