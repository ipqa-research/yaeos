full_newton

Description

Correct one predicted continuation point with Newton’s method. The correction solves at fixed specification value, halving the Newton step until . It stops when or tol. If iteration 10 is reached (for points after the first), is reset to the previous point plus 10% of the predictor step. On failure iters ends at max_iters + 1.
