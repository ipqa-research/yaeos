public pure subroutine bmix_qmr(n, bi, lij, b, dbi, dbij)

bmix_qmr

Calculate the QMR covolume and its first and second mole derivatives.

Description

Forms pair covolumes and evaluates . The derivatives are returned with respect to the component mole amounts.

Examples

call bmix_qmr(n, bi, lij, B, dBdn, d2Bdn2)
