Solvers Fitting module

These helpers compute the phase-equilibrium states that the fitter compares with experimental data. They answer a different question from the flash methods in Core Fitting module: they return the compositions of two phases at given pressure and temperature, not the phase amounts of an overall feed.

solve_pt(model, pressure, temperature, kind)

Solves a binary equilibrium at the given pressure (bar) and temperature (K). It handles liquid-vapor and liquid-liquid cases; the kind argument selects which ("liquid-liquid" or "PT").

binary_isofugacity_x1y1pt(x, p, t, model)

Evaluates the binary isofugacity condition. solve_pt does not call it (it runs flash_pt on a feed between the two initial guesses); it is available for building custom solvers. The argument x is the pair of trial mole fractions [y1, x1] of the light component, and the result is the squared residual of the equal-fugacity condition.

find_init_binary_ll(model, pressure, temperature, kind)

Provides an initial guess for the liquid-liquid case by scanning the fugacity of the light component over the composition range.

Interpreting the output

The solver returns (x1, y1): the mole fraction of the light component in the heavy and the light phase. The complete binary compositions are [x1, 1 - x1] and [y1, 1 - y1] in the model’s component order. A solution with x1 == y1 means no separation was found at those conditions, which the fitter interprets as a single-phase situation rather than as an error.

Cautions

Initial guesses and near-identical phase compositions make these solves fragile. Check that the returned values are finite and physically plausible (0 <= x1, y1 <= 1) and that the branch corresponds to the phase pair you expect. A small isofugacity residual alone does not guarantee the correct branch was followed.

These functions are used by Core Fitting module for the PT and liquid-liquid data kinds; see the fitting tutorial for how they fit into a complete workflow.

solve_pt(model: ArModel, pressure: float, temperature: float, kind: str) → tuple[float, float][source]

Solve a point at a given P and T.

This function solves a point at a given pressure and temperature. That is, solve the composition of two phases in equilibrium at T and P. The function handles liquid-vapor or liquid-liquid.

Parameters:

  • model (ArModel) – yaeos ArModel object.

  • pressure (float) – Pressure [bar].

  • temperature (float) – Temperature [K].

  • kind (str) –

    Kind of phase equilibrium calculation. Options are:

    • ”liquid-liquid”: liquid-liquid equilibrium calculation.

    • ”PT”: pressure-temperature calculation.

Returns:

x1, y1: Mole fractions of component 1 (light component) in both phases.

Return type:

tuple[float, float]
